Creamy Hot Chocolate

A cosy cocoa drink topped with marshmallows.

Beginner friendly · Easy-to-follow steps

Ingredients

  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 tbsp cocoa powder
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 25 g chopped chocolate
  • Marshmallows, optional

Method

  1. Whisk cocoa and sugar with a splash of milk in a saucepan.
  2. Add the remaining milk and warm gently, stirring.
  3. Add chopped chocolate and stir until melted.
  4. Pour into a mug and top as desired.
  5. Avoid boiling the milk.

Kitchen note: Read the full method before you begin. Use care with hot pans, ovens and blenders, and ask an experienced adult for help when needed.
