Masala Omelette

An easy omelette with onion, tomato and herbs.

Beginner friendly · Easy-to-follow steps

Ingredients

  • 2 eggs
  • 2 tbsp chopped onion
  • 2 tbsp chopped tomato
  • 1 tbsp chopped coriander
  • 1 green chilli, optional
  • Salt and pepper
  • 1 tsp oil

Method

  1. Beat eggs with salt and pepper.
  2. Stir in onion, tomato, coriander and optional chilli.
  3. Heat oil in a pan over medium-low heat.
  4. Pour in the egg mixture and cook until the base sets.
  5. Fold and cook until fully set.

Kitchen note: Read the full method before you begin. Use care with hot pans, ovens and blenders, and ask an experienced adult for help when needed.
