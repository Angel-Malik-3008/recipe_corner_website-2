Baked Cheese Balls

Crisp little bites with a cheesy centre.

Beginner friendly · Easy-to-follow steps

Ingredients

  • 1 cup mashed boiled potato
  • 1/2 cup grated cheese
  • 2 tbsp breadcrumbs
  • 1 tbsp chopped coriander
  • Salt and pepper
  • Extra breadcrumbs for coating

Method

  1. Mix potato, cheese, breadcrumbs and coriander.
  2. Season and shape into small balls.
  3. Roll each ball in extra breadcrumbs.
  4. Place on a lined tray and lightly brush with oil.
  5. Bake at 200°C for 15–20 minutes, turning once.

Kitchen note: Read the full method before you begin. Use care with hot pans, ovens and blenders, and ask an experienced adult for help when needed.
