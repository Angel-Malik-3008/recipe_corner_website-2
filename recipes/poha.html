Vegetable Poha

A light Indian breakfast with peanuts and curry leaves.

Beginner friendly · Easy-to-follow steps

Ingredients

  • 1 cup thick poha
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 2 tbsp peanuts
  • 1/2 tsp mustard seeds
  • 8 curry leaves
  • 1/4 cup peas
  • 1/4 tsp turmeric
  • Lemon juice

Method

  1. Rinse poha briefly in a sieve and let it soften.
  2. Heat oil; add mustard seeds, peanuts and curry leaves.
  3. Add peas and turmeric; cook until peas are tender.
  4. Fold in poha and salt; warm gently for 2–3 minutes.
  5. Finish with lemon juice and serve.

Kitchen note: Read the full method before you begin. Use care with hot pans, ovens and blenders, and ask an experienced adult for help when needed.
