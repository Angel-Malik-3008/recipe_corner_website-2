Lemon Icebox Pie

A bright, tangy chilled pie with a buttery crumb crust.

Beginner friendly · No oven needed

Ingredients

  • 1 prepared graham cracker crust
  • 1 can sweetened condensed milk
  • 1/2 cup fresh lemon juice
  • 1 tbsp lemon zest
  • 1 cup whipped cream

Method

  1. Whisk condensed milk, lemon juice and zest until thickened.
  2. Fold in the whipped cream until smooth.
  3. Pour into the crust and smooth the top.
  4. Refrigerate for at least 4 hours, until set.
  5. Slice and serve chilled.

Kitchen note: Read the full method before you begin. Keep chilled ingredients refrigerated until serving.
