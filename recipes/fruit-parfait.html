Fruit Yoghurt Parfait

Layers of yoghurt, fruit and crunchy granola.

Beginner friendly · Easy-to-follow steps

Ingredients

  • 1 cup thick yoghurt
  • 1 tsp honey
  • 1/2 cup chopped seasonal fruit
  • 1/4 cup granola
  • A few nuts

Method

  1. Stir honey into the yoghurt.
  2. Spoon a layer of yoghurt into a glass.
  3. Add fruit and granola.
  4. Repeat the layers and finish with nuts.
  5. Serve straight away so the granola stays crunchy.

Kitchen note: Read the full method before you begin. Use care with hot pans, ovens and blenders, and ask an experienced adult for help when needed.
