Chocolate Avocado Mousse

A silky cocoa mousse blended smooth and chilled until cool.

Beginner friendly · No oven needed

Ingredients

  • 2 ripe avocados
  • 1/3 cup cocoa powder
  • 1/3 cup maple syrup or honey
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 2–4 tbsp milk, as needed

Method

  1. Scoop the avocado flesh into a blender.
  2. Add cocoa, syrup and vanilla.
  3. Blend until completely smooth, adding a little milk if needed.
  4. Spoon into small cups.
  5. Chill for 30 minutes before serving.

Kitchen note: Read the full method before you begin. Keep chilled ingredients refrigerated until serving.
