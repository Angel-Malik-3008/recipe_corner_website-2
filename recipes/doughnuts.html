Baked Doughnuts

Soft ring-shaped treats finished with a simple glaze.

Beginner friendly · Easy-to-follow steps

Ingredients

  • 1 cup flour
  • 1/4 cup sugar
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 1 egg
  • 2 tbsp melted butter
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C and grease a doughnut pan.
  2. Mix flour, sugar and baking powder.
  3. Whisk milk, egg, butter and vanilla in another bowl.
  4. Combine the mixtures until smooth.
  5. Pipe into the pan and bake for 10–12 minutes.
  6. Cool, then dip in a simple icing or cinnamon sugar.

Kitchen note: Read the full method before you begin. Use care with hot pans, ovens and blenders, and ask an experienced adult for help when needed.
