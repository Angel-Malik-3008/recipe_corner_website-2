Chocolate Oat Bites

No-bake cocoa and oat bites for a quick sweet snack.

Beginner friendly · No oven needed

Ingredients

  • 1 cup rolled oats
  • 1/2 cup sunflower-seed butter
  • 1/4 cup honey
  • 2 tbsp cocoa powder
  • 2 tbsp mini chocolate chips

Method

  1. Mix oats and cocoa in a bowl.
  2. Stir in sunflower-seed butter and honey.
  3. Fold in the chocolate chips.
  4. Roll into small balls with clean hands.
  5. Refrigerate for 20 minutes to firm up.

Kitchen note: Read the full method before you begin. Keep chilled ingredients refrigerated until serving.
