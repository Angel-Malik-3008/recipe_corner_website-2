Crispy Potato Wedges

Seasoned potato wedges baked until golden.

Beginner friendly · Easy-to-follow steps

Ingredients

  • 2 medium potatoes
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1/2 tsp paprika
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • Salt and pepper

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 220°C.
  2. Wash potatoes and cut into wedges.
  3. Toss with oil and seasonings.
  4. Spread in a single layer on a lined tray.
  5. Bake 25–35 minutes, turning once, until tender and golden.

Kitchen note: Read the full method before you begin. Use care with hot pans, ovens and blenders, and ask an experienced adult for help when needed.
