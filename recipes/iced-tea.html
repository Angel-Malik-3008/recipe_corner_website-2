Peach Iced Tea

Light tea with peach and a squeeze of lemon.

Beginner friendly · Easy-to-follow steps

Ingredients

  • 2 tea bags
  • 2 cups hot water
  • 1/2 cup peach juice or purée
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • Ice

Method

  1. Steep tea bags in hot water for 3–4 minutes, then remove.
  2. Cool the tea completely.
  3. Stir in peach juice, honey and lemon.
  4. Pour over ice.
  5. Keep chilled until serving.

Kitchen note: Read the full method before you begin. Use care with hot pans, ovens and blenders, and ask an experienced adult for help when needed.
