Vegetable Sandwich

A fresh, crunchy sandwich with a quick spread.

Beginner friendly · Easy-to-follow steps

Ingredients

  • 4 bread slices
  • 1/4 cup grated carrot
  • 1/4 cup chopped cucumber
  • 1/4 cup chopped capsicum
  • 2 tbsp hung curd or mayonnaise
  • Salt and pepper

Method

  1. Mix the vegetables with hung curd or mayonnaise.
  2. Season lightly with salt and pepper.
  3. Spread the filling over two bread slices.
  4. Cover with the remaining slices and press gently.
  5. Cut in halves and serve fresh.

Kitchen note: Read the full method before you begin. Use care with hot pans, ovens and blenders, and ask an experienced adult for help when needed.
