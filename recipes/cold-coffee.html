Classic Cold Coffee

Chilled coffee blended until frothy.

Beginner friendly · Easy-to-follow steps

Ingredients

  • 1 cup chilled milk
  • 1 tsp instant coffee
  • 1–2 tsp sugar
  • 3 ice cubes
  • 1 scoop vanilla ice cream, optional

Method

  1. Dissolve coffee in 1 tablespoon warm water.
  2. Add coffee, milk, sugar and ice to a blender.
  3. Blend until frothy.
  4. Pour into a glass and add ice cream if desired.
  5. Serve immediately.

Kitchen note: Read the full method before you begin. Use care with hot pans, ovens and blenders, and ask an experienced adult for help when needed.
