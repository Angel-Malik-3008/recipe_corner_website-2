Chocolate Mousse

A fluffy chocolate dessert served chilled.

Beginner friendly · Easy-to-follow steps

Ingredients

  • 150 g semi-sweet chocolate
  • 1 cup chilled whipping cream
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla essence

Method

  1. Melt the chocolate gently and let it cool until lukewarm.
  2. Whip the cream with sugar and vanilla to soft peaks.
  3. Fold a little cream into the chocolate, then gently fold in the rest.
  4. Divide into small glasses.
  5. Refrigerate for at least 2 hours before serving.

Kitchen note: Read the full method before you begin. Use care with hot pans, ovens and blenders, and ask an experienced adult for help when needed.
