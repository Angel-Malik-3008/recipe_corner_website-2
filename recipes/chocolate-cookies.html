Chocolate Chip Cookies

Golden cookies with chocolate in every bite.

Beginner friendly · Easy-to-follow steps

Ingredients

  • 1 cup flour
  • 1/2 cup softened butter
  • 1/2 cup brown sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 1/2 cup chocolate chips
  • 1/2 tsp baking soda

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 180°C and line a tray.
  2. Cream butter and sugar, then mix in the egg.
  3. Fold in flour and baking soda.
  4. Stir through chocolate chips and place spoonfuls on the tray.
  5. Bake 10–12 minutes; cool on the tray briefly.

Kitchen note: Read the full method before you begin. Use care with hot pans, ovens and blenders, and ask an experienced adult for help when needed.
