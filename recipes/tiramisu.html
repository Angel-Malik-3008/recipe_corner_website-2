Easy Tiramisu

Coffee-soaked layers with a light creamy filling.

Beginner friendly · Easy-to-follow steps

Ingredients

  • 12 sponge fingers or plain biscuits
  • 1/2 cup cooled strong coffee
  • 200 g mascarpone or thick cream cheese
  • 1/2 cup whipped cream
  • 3 tbsp powdered sugar
  • Cocoa powder for dusting

Method

  1. Stir the sugar into the mascarpone until smooth.
  2. Fold in the whipped cream.
  3. Briefly dip biscuits in cooled coffee; do not soak them.
  4. Layer biscuits and cream in a dish or individual glasses.
  5. Chill for 4 hours, then dust with cocoa powder.

Kitchen note: Read the full method before you begin. Use care with hot pans, ovens and blenders, and ask an experienced adult for help when needed.
