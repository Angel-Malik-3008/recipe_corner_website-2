Oat and Date Energy Balls

Small naturally sweet bites with oats and dates.

Beginner friendly · Easy-to-follow steps

Ingredients

  • 1 cup soft pitted dates
  • 1/2 cup rolled oats
  • 2 tbsp peanut butter
  • 1 tbsp cocoa powder
  • 2 tbsp chopped nuts

Method

  1. Blend dates into a sticky paste.
  2. Add oats, peanut butter and cocoa.
  3. Mix until the mixture holds together.
  4. Roll into small balls and coat with chopped nuts.
  5. Chill for 30 minutes before serving.

Kitchen note: Read the full method before you begin. Use care with hot pans, ovens and blenders, and ask an experienced adult for help when needed.
