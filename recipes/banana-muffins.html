Banana Muffins

A tender bake made with ripe bananas.

Beginner friendly · Easy-to-follow steps

Ingredients

  • 2 ripe bananas, mashed
  • 1 cup flour
  • 1/3 cup sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 1/4 cup oil
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp cinnamon

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C and line a muffin tray.
  2. Mash bananas and whisk in egg, sugar and oil.
  3. Fold in flour, baking powder and cinnamon.
  4. Divide into muffin cases.
  5. Bake for 18–22 minutes and cool before eating.

Kitchen note: Read the full method before you begin. Use care with hot pans, ovens and blenders, and ask an experienced adult for help when needed.
