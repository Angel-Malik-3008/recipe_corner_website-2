Banana Shake

A smooth and filling banana drink.

Beginner friendly · Easy-to-follow steps

Ingredients

  • 1 ripe banana
  • 1 cup chilled milk
  • 1 tsp honey or sugar, optional
  • A pinch of cinnamon

Method

  1. Peel and slice the banana.
  2. Add banana and milk to a blender.
  3. Blend until smooth.
  4. Add honey or cinnamon if desired.
  5. Serve chilled.

Kitchen note: Read the full method before you begin. Use care with hot pans, ovens and blenders, and ask an experienced adult for help when needed.
