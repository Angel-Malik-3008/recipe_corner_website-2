Creamy Scrambled Eggs

A quick savoury breakfast favourite.

Beginner friendly · Easy-to-follow steps

Ingredients

  • 2 eggs
  • 2 tbsp milk
  • 1 tsp butter
  • A pinch of salt
  • Black pepper

Method

  1. Whisk eggs with milk, salt and pepper.
  2. Melt butter in a non-stick pan over low heat.
  3. Add eggs and stir slowly with a spatula.
  4. Remove from heat while still softly set; residual heat finishes cooking.
  5. Serve immediately with toast.

Kitchen note: Read the full method before you begin. Use care with hot pans, ovens and blenders, and ask an experienced adult for help when needed.
