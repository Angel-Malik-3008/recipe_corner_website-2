Oreo Truffles

Creamy cookie truffles with a chocolate coating.

Beginner friendly · Easy-to-follow steps

Ingredients

  • 18 chocolate sandwich cookies
  • 100 g cream cheese, softened
  • 150 g melted chocolate

Method

  1. Crush cookies into fine crumbs.
  2. Mix crumbs with cream cheese until combined.
  3. Roll into small balls and chill for 30 minutes.
  4. Dip each ball in melted chocolate.
  5. Refrigerate until the coating sets.

Kitchen note: Read the full method before you begin. Use care with hot pans, ovens and blenders, and ask an experienced adult for help when needed.
