Vegetable Spring Rolls

Crisp rolls filled with lightly seasoned vegetables.

Beginner friendly · Easy-to-follow steps

Ingredients

  • 8 spring-roll wrappers
  • 1 cup shredded cabbage
  • 1/2 cup grated carrot
  • 1/4 cup sliced capsicum
  • 1 tsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp oil

Method

  1. Cook vegetables in a pan with oil for 2–3 minutes.
  2. Stir in soy sauce and let the filling cool.
  3. Place filling on each wrapper and fold the sides inward.
  4. Roll tightly and seal the edge with a little water.
  5. Bake at 200°C until crisp, turning once.

Kitchen note: Read the full method before you begin. Use care with hot pans, ovens and blenders, and ask an experienced adult for help when needed.
