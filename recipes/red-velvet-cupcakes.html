Red Velvet Cupcakes

Soft cocoa cupcakes with a creamy topping.

Beginner friendly · Easy-to-follow steps

Ingredients

  • 1 cup flour
  • 3/4 cup sugar
  • 1 tbsp cocoa powder
  • 1/2 tsp baking soda
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 1/3 cup oil
  • 1 tsp vinegar
  • Red food colouring (optional)

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C and line a cupcake tray.
  2. Combine flour, sugar, cocoa and baking soda.
  3. Mix milk, oil, vinegar and colouring separately.
  4. Combine wet and dry ingredients without overmixing.
  5. Fill cupcake cases halfway and bake for 16–20 minutes.
  6. Cool completely before adding your favourite frosting.

Kitchen note: Read the full method before you begin. Use care with hot pans, ovens and blenders, and ask an experienced adult for help when needed.
