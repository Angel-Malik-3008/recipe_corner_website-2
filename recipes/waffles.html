Golden Waffles

Crisp-edged waffles for a dessert-style treat.

Beginner friendly · Easy-to-follow steps

Ingredients

  • 1 cup flour
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 3/4 cup milk
  • 1 egg
  • 2 tbsp melted butter

Method

  1. Preheat and lightly grease a waffle maker.
  2. Mix flour, sugar and baking powder.
  3. Whisk milk, egg and butter; stir into the dry ingredients.
  4. Pour batter into the waffle maker without overfilling.
  5. Cook until golden and serve with fruit or syrup.

Kitchen note: Read the full method before you begin. Use care with hot pans, ovens and blenders, and ask an experienced adult for help when needed.
