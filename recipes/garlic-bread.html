Cheesy Garlic Bread

Toasty bread with garlic butter and melted cheese.

Beginner friendly · Easy-to-follow steps

Ingredients

  • 1 small baguette or 4 bread slices
  • 2 tbsp softened butter
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley
  • 1/2 cup grated cheese

Method

  1. Mix butter, garlic and parsley.
  2. Spread over the cut bread.
  3. Sprinkle with grated cheese.
  4. Bake at 180°C for 8–10 minutes until crisp and melted.
  5. Serve warm.

Kitchen note: Read the full method before you begin. Use care with hot pans, ovens and blenders, and ask an experienced adult for help when needed.
