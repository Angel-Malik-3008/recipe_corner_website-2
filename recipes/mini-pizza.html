Mini Vegetable Pizzas

Small pizzas with a simple vegetable topping.

Beginner friendly · Easy-to-follow steps

Ingredients

  • 4 small bread rounds or English muffin halves
  • 1/3 cup pizza sauce
  • 1/2 cup grated mozzarella
  • 1/4 cup chopped capsicum
  • 2 tbsp sweet corn
  • Dried oregano

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 190°C.
  2. Spread pizza sauce over each bread round.
  3. Add cheese, capsicum and corn.
  4. Sprinkle with oregano.
  5. Bake for 8–10 minutes until the cheese melts.

Kitchen note: Read the full method before you begin. Use care with hot pans, ovens and blenders, and ask an experienced adult for help when needed.
