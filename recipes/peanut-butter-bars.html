Peanut Butter Oat Bars

Chewy fridge bars with peanut butter and oats.

Beginner friendly · Easy-to-follow steps

Ingredients

  • 1 cup rolled oats
  • 1/2 cup peanut butter
  • 1/4 cup honey
  • 2 tbsp melted chocolate, optional

Method

  1. Stir peanut butter and honey until smooth.
  2. Mix in the oats until evenly coated.
  3. Press firmly into a lined small container.
  4. Drizzle with melted chocolate if using.
  5. Refrigerate for 1–2 hours before slicing.

Kitchen note: Read the full method before you begin. Use care with hot pans, ovens and blenders, and ask an experienced adult for help when needed.
