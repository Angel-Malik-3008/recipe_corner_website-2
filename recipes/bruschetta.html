Tomato Bruschetta

Toasted bread topped with fresh tomato and herbs.

Beginner friendly · Easy-to-follow steps

Ingredients

  • 1 small baguette
  • 2 ripe tomatoes, diced
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp chopped basil
  • 1 garlic clove
  • Salt and pepper

Method

  1. Toast baguette slices until crisp.
  2. Mix tomatoes with olive oil, basil, salt and pepper.
  3. Rub the warm toast lightly with the garlic clove.
  4. Spoon tomato mixture onto each slice.
  5. Serve immediately.

Kitchen note: Read the full method before you begin. Use care with hot pans, ovens and blenders, and ask an experienced adult for help when needed.
