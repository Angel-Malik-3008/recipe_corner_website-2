Masala Popcorn

A quick bowl of popcorn with a savoury spice mix.

Beginner friendly · Easy-to-follow steps

Ingredients

  • 1/3 cup popcorn kernels
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1/4 tsp chaat masala
  • A pinch of turmeric
  • Salt to taste

Method

  1. Warm oil in a large lidded pot over medium heat.
  2. Add kernels and cover, leaving a small vent if the lid allows.
  3. Shake the pot gently as the kernels pop.
  4. When popping slows, remove from heat.
  5. Season carefully with chaat masala, turmeric and salt.

Kitchen note: Read the full method before you begin. Use care with hot pans, ovens and blenders, and ask an experienced adult for help when needed.
