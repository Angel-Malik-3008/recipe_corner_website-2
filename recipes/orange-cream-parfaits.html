Orange Cream Parfaits

Bright orange and vanilla cream layered into chilled glasses.

Beginner friendly · No oven needed

Ingredients

  • 1 cup orange segments, chopped
  • 1 cup thick vanilla yogurt
  • 1/2 cup whipped cream
  • 1 tsp orange zest
  • 1/2 cup crushed vanilla biscuits

Method

  1. Fold whipped cream into the yogurt.
  2. Stir orange zest into the cream mixture.
  3. Add a layer of biscuit crumbs to each glass.
  4. Layer in orange pieces and orange cream.
  5. Chill for 30 minutes before serving.

Kitchen note: Read the full method before you begin. Keep chilled ingredients refrigerated until serving.
