Easy Baked Samosas

A crisp pastry filled with spiced potatoes and peas.

Beginner friendly · Easy-to-follow steps

Ingredients

  • 6 small samosa pastry sheets
  • 2 boiled potatoes, mashed
  • 1/4 cup peas
  • 1/2 tsp cumin
  • 1/2 tsp garam masala
  • Salt
  • A little oil

Method

  1. Mix potato, peas, cumin, garam masala and salt.
  2. Fill and fold pastry sheets into triangles, sealing the edges with water.
  3. Place on a lined tray and brush lightly with oil.
  4. Bake at 200°C for 18–22 minutes, turning once.
  5. Serve with chutney.

Kitchen note: Read the full method before you begin. Use care with hot pans, ovens and blenders, and ask an experienced adult for help when needed.
