Chocolate Milkshake

A classic chocolate drink with a creamy finish.

Beginner friendly · Easy-to-follow steps

Ingredients

  • 1 cup chilled milk
  • 2 scoops chocolate ice cream
  • 1 tbsp cocoa powder
  • 1 tsp chocolate syrup

Method

  1. Add milk, ice cream and cocoa to a blender.
  2. Blend until smooth.
  3. Drizzle chocolate syrup inside a glass.
  4. Pour in the milkshake and serve chilled.

Kitchen note: Read the full method before you begin. Use care with hot pans, ovens and blenders, and ask an experienced adult for help when needed.
