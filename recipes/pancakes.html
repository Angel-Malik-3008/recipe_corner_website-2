Fluffy Pancakes

A soft breakfast classic with your favourite toppings.

Beginner friendly · Easy-to-follow steps

Ingredients

  • 1 cup flour
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 3/4 cup milk
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tbsp melted butter

Method

  1. Mix flour, sugar and baking powder.
  2. Whisk milk, egg and butter in another bowl.
  3. Combine gently; a few small lumps are fine.
  4. Pour small rounds onto a lightly greased warm pan.
  5. Flip when bubbles appear and cook the other side until golden.

Kitchen note: Read the full method before you begin. Use care with hot pans, ovens and blenders, and ask an experienced adult for help when needed.
