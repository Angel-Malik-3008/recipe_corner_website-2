Pistachio Date Rolls

Soft date rolls with a nutty pistachio coating.

Beginner friendly · No oven needed

Ingredients

  • 1 cup soft pitted dates
  • 1/2 cup shelled pistachios
  • 1/2 cup desiccated coconut
  • 1 tbsp cocoa powder, optional

Method

  1. Finely chop the pistachios and set aside.
  2. Blend dates and coconut until they form a sticky mixture.
  3. Add cocoa if using and pulse to combine.
  4. Roll into small logs or balls.
  5. Coat with chopped pistachios and chill.

Kitchen note: Read the full method before you begin. Keep chilled ingredients refrigerated until serving.
