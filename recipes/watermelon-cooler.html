Watermelon Cooler

A refreshing watermelon drink with lime.

Beginner friendly · Easy-to-follow steps

Ingredients

  • 2 cups seedless watermelon cubes
  • 1 tsp lime juice
  • 4 mint leaves
  • A few ice cubes

Method

  1. Chill the watermelon cubes.
  2. Blend watermelon until smooth.
  3. Strain if you prefer a smoother drink.
  4. Stir in lime juice and gently crushed mint.
  5. Serve over ice.

Kitchen note: Read the full method before you begin. Use care with hot pans, ovens and blenders, and ask an experienced adult for help when needed.
