Mango Dessert Cups

Layers of mango, yoghurt and crunchy crumbs.

Beginner friendly · Easy-to-follow steps

Ingredients

  • 1 ripe mango, diced
  • 1 cup thick sweetened yoghurt
  • 1/2 cup crushed biscuits
  • 1 tbsp chopped pistachios

Method

  1. Prepare mango pieces and keep a few aside for topping.
  2. Add a layer of biscuit crumbs to each glass.
  3. Spoon in yoghurt, then add mango.
  4. Repeat the layers if the glass allows.
  5. Finish with pistachios and chill until serving.

Kitchen note: Read the full method before you begin. Use care with hot pans, ovens and blenders, and ask an experienced adult for help when needed.
