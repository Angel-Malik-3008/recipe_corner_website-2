Strawberry Yogurt Bark

A refreshing frozen yogurt treat with strawberries and a little crunch.

Beginner friendly · No oven needed

Ingredients

  • 2 cups thick yogurt
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla
  • 1 cup sliced strawberries
  • 2 tbsp granola

Method

  1. Line a tray with baking paper.
  2. Stir yogurt with honey and vanilla.
  3. Spread in an even layer on the tray.
  4. Scatter strawberries and granola over the top.
  5. Freeze until firm, then break into pieces.

Kitchen note: Read the full method before you begin. Keep chilled ingredients refrigerated until serving.
