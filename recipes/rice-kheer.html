Rice Kheer

A comforting Indian rice pudding with cardamom.

Beginner friendly · Easy-to-follow steps

Ingredients

  • 1/4 cup basmati rice
  • 1 litre milk
  • 4 tbsp sugar
  • 2 green cardamom pods, crushed
  • 2 tbsp chopped nuts

Method

  1. Rinse and soak the rice for 15 minutes, then drain.
  2. Bring milk to a gentle simmer in a heavy pan.
  3. Add rice and cook on low, stirring often, until soft and creamy.
  4. Stir in sugar and cardamom; simmer for a few more minutes.
  5. Top with nuts and serve warm or chilled.

Kitchen note: Read the full method before you begin. Use care with hot pans, ovens and blenders, and ask an experienced adult for help when needed.
