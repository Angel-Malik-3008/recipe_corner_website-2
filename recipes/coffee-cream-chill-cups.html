Coffee Cream Chill Cups

A coffee-flavoured chilled dessert with a crisp biscuit base.

Beginner friendly · No oven needed

Ingredients

  • 1 cup crushed plain biscuits
  • 2 tbsp melted butter
  • 1 cup chilled whipped cream
  • 2 tbsp strong cooled coffee
  • 2 tbsp powdered sugar

Method

  1. Mix biscuit crumbs with melted butter and divide into cups.
  2. Stir cooled coffee and powdered sugar into the whipped cream.
  3. Spoon coffee cream over the crumb bases.
  4. Dust lightly with cocoa if desired.
  5. Chill for at least 1 hour before serving.

Kitchen note: Read the full method before you begin. Keep chilled ingredients refrigerated until serving.
