Banana Pudding Jars

Creamy banana pudding layered with biscuits in individual jars.

Beginner friendly · No oven needed

Ingredients

  • 2 ripe bananas
  • 1 cup vanilla pudding, prepared and chilled
  • 1 cup whipped cream
  • 12 vanilla wafers or plain biscuits
  • 1 tsp lemon juice

Method

  1. Slice one banana and toss lightly with lemon juice.
  2. Mash the other banana and fold into the pudding.
  3. Layer biscuit pieces, pudding and banana slices in jars.
  4. Top with whipped cream.
  5. Chill for 1 hour before serving.

Kitchen note: Read the full method before you begin. Keep chilled ingredients refrigerated until serving.
