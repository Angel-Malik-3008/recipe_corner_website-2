Strawberry Smoothie

A fruity, creamy drink for any time of day.

Beginner friendly · Easy-to-follow steps

Ingredients

  • 1 cup strawberries
  • 1 banana
  • 3/4 cup milk
  • 1/4 cup yoghurt
  • Honey, optional

Method

  1. Wash and hull the strawberries.
  2. Add strawberries, banana, milk and yoghurt to a blender.
  3. Blend until smooth.
  4. Taste and add a little honey if desired.
  5. Pour into a glass and serve chilled.

Kitchen note: Read the full method before you begin. Use care with hot pans, ovens and blenders, and ask an experienced adult for help when needed.
