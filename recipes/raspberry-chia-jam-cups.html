Raspberry Chia Cups

A fruity layered dessert with quick chia jam and creamy yogurt.

Beginner friendly · No oven needed

Ingredients

  • 1 cup raspberries, fresh or thawed
  • 1 tbsp chia seeds
  • 1 tsp maple syrup
  • 1 cup thick yogurt
  • 1/2 cup crushed plain biscuits

Method

  1. Mash raspberries with a fork.
  2. Stir in chia seeds and maple syrup; rest for 15 minutes to thicken.
  3. Layer crushed biscuits and yogurt in serving cups.
  4. Spoon raspberry chia jam on top.
  5. Chill briefly before serving.

Kitchen note: Read the full method before you begin. Keep chilled ingredients refrigerated until serving.
