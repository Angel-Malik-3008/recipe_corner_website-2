Banana Breakfast Muffins

Portable muffins for busy mornings.

Beginner friendly · Easy-to-follow steps

Ingredients

  • 2 ripe bananas
  • 1 cup flour
  • 1 egg
  • 1/4 cup milk
  • 1/4 cup oil
  • 1 tsp baking powder

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 180°C and line a muffin tray.
  2. Mash bananas and mix with egg, milk and oil.
  3. Fold in flour and baking powder.
  4. Divide into cases and bake for 18–22 minutes.
  5. Cool before storing in an airtight container.

Kitchen note: Read the full method before you begin. Use care with hot pans, ovens and blenders, and ask an experienced adult for help when needed.
