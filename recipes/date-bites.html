Cocoa Date Bites

Naturally sweet date and nut bites.

Beginner friendly · Easy-to-follow steps

Ingredients

  • 1 cup soft pitted dates
  • 1/2 cup almonds or cashews
  • 1 tbsp cocoa powder
  • 1 tbsp desiccated coconut

Method

  1. Blend dates and nuts until finely chopped and sticky.
  2. Add cocoa powder and pulse to combine.
  3. Shape into small bite-sized balls.
  4. Roll in coconut.
  5. Chill briefly to firm up.

Kitchen note: Read the full method before you begin. Use care with hot pans, ovens and blenders, and ask an experienced adult for help when needed.
