Mango Milkshake

A thick summer favourite made with ripe mango.

Beginner friendly · Easy-to-follow steps

Ingredients

  • 1 ripe mango, peeled and chopped
  • 1 cup chilled milk
  • 1 scoop vanilla ice cream, optional
  • A pinch of cardamom, optional

Method

  1. Add mango and chilled milk to a blender.
  2. Blend until smooth and creamy.
  3. Add ice cream or cardamom if desired.
  4. Pour into glasses and serve immediately.

Kitchen note: Read the full method before you begin. Use care with hot pans, ovens and blenders, and ask an experienced adult for help when needed.
