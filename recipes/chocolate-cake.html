Chocolate Cake

Rich, soft and full of chocolate.

Beginner friendly · Easy-to-follow steps

Ingredients

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 cup cocoa powder
  • 3/4 cup sugar
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 1/4 cup neutral oil
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla essence

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C and grease a small cake tin.
  2. Whisk flour, cocoa powder, sugar and baking powder in a bowl.
  3. Add milk, oil and vanilla; mix until just combined.
  4. Pour into the tin and bake for 25–30 minutes, until a toothpick comes out mostly clean.
  5. Cool before slicing and serving.

Kitchen note: Read the full method before you begin. Use care with hot pans, ovens and blenders, and ask an experienced adult for help when needed.
