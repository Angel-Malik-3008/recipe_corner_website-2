Apple Crumble Pie

Warm cinnamon apples under a buttery crumb topping.

Beginner friendly · Easy-to-follow steps

Ingredients

  • 2 apples, peeled and sliced
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 1/2 tsp cinnamon
  • 1/2 cup flour
  • 3 tbsp cold butter
  • 2 tbsp oats

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C.
  2. Toss apple slices with half the sugar and cinnamon; place in a small baking dish.
  3. Rub butter into flour, oats and remaining sugar until crumbly.
  4. Scatter the topping over the apples.
  5. Bake for 25–30 minutes until golden and bubbling.

Kitchen note: Read the full method before you begin. Use care with hot pans, ovens and blenders, and ask an experienced adult for help when needed.
