Chocolate Biscuit Cake

A chilled chocolate slice made with biscuits.

Beginner friendly · Easy-to-follow steps

Ingredients

  • 200 g plain biscuits, broken
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 2 tbsp cocoa powder
  • 3 tbsp sugar
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla

Method

  1. Warm milk, cocoa, sugar and butter in a saucepan until combined; let cool.
  2. Break biscuits into bite-sized pieces in a bowl.
  3. Pour the cooled cocoa mixture over the biscuits.
  4. Press into a lined small tin.
  5. Refrigerate for 4 hours or until firm, then slice.

Kitchen note: Read the full method before you begin. Use care with hot pans, ovens and blenders, and ask an experienced adult for help when needed.
