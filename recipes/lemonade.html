Fresh Lemonade

A simple sweet-and-tangy cooler.

Beginner friendly · Easy-to-follow steps

Ingredients

  • 2 lemons
  • 3 cups chilled water
  • 2–3 tbsp sugar
  • A pinch of salt
  • Mint and ice

Method

  1. Squeeze the lemons into a jug and remove the seeds.
  2. Stir in sugar and salt until dissolved.
  3. Add chilled water and mix.
  4. Taste and adjust sweetness.
  5. Serve with mint and ice.

Kitchen note: Read the full method before you begin. Use care with hot pans, ovens and blenders, and ask an experienced adult for help when needed.
