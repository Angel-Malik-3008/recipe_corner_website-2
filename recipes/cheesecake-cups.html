No-Bake Cheesecake Cups

Individual creamy cups with a biscuit base.

Beginner friendly · Easy-to-follow steps

Ingredients

  • 1 cup biscuit crumbs
  • 2 tbsp melted butter
  • 200 g cream cheese
  • 1/3 cup powdered sugar
  • 1/2 cup whipped cream

Method

  1. Combine biscuit crumbs and butter; divide between cups.
  2. Beat cream cheese and sugar until smooth.
  3. Fold in whipped cream.
  4. Spoon over the biscuit base.
  5. Chill for at least 2 hours and add fruit before serving.

Kitchen note: Read the full method before you begin. Use care with hot pans, ovens and blenders, and ask an experienced adult for help when needed.
