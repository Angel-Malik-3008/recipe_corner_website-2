Oreo Milkshake

A fun cookies-and-cream milkshake.

Beginner friendly · Easy-to-follow steps

Ingredients

  • 4 chocolate sandwich cookies
  • 1 cup chilled milk
  • 2 scoops vanilla ice cream
  • Extra cookie crumbs

Method

  1. Add cookies, milk and ice cream to a blender.
  2. Blend until creamy, leaving a few small cookie pieces if preferred.
  3. Pour into a glass.
  4. Top with cookie crumbs and serve immediately.

Kitchen note: Read the full method before you begin. Use care with hot pans, ovens and blenders, and ask an experienced adult for help when needed.
