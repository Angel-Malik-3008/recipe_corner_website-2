Coconut Ladoos

Quick coconut sweets with a soft, chewy texture.

Beginner friendly · Easy-to-follow steps

Ingredients

  • 2 cups desiccated coconut
  • 3/4 cup condensed milk
  • 1/4 tsp cardamom powder
  • Extra coconut for rolling

Method

  1. Set aside a few tablespoons of coconut for coating.
  2. Mix remaining coconut with condensed milk and cardamom.
  3. Chill briefly if the mixture feels sticky.
  4. Shape into small balls.
  5. Roll in the reserved coconut and refrigerate.

Kitchen note: Read the full method before you begin. Use care with hot pans, ovens and blenders, and ask an experienced adult for help when needed.
