Avocado Toast

Creamy avocado on crisp toasted bread.

Beginner friendly · Easy-to-follow steps

Ingredients

  • 2 bread slices
  • 1 ripe avocado
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • A pinch of salt
  • Black pepper
  • Cherry tomatoes (optional)

Method

  1. Toast the bread until crisp.
  2. Mash avocado with lemon juice, salt and pepper.
  3. Spread the avocado over the toast.
  4. Top with sliced tomatoes if desired.
  5. Serve immediately.

Kitchen note: Read the full method before you begin. Use care with hot pans, ovens and blenders, and ask an experienced adult for help when needed.
