Chilled Fruit Custard

A creamy chilled dessert with fresh fruit.

Beginner friendly · Easy-to-follow steps

Ingredients

  • 2 cups chilled prepared custard
  • 1 cup chopped seasonal fruit
  • 1 tbsp chopped nuts
  • A few pomegranate seeds

Method

  1. Prepare custard according to its packet directions and chill completely.
  2. Wash and chop the fruit into bite-sized pieces.
  3. Fold fruit into the cold custard just before serving.
  4. Top with nuts and pomegranate.
  5. Keep refrigerated until serving.

Kitchen note: Read the full method before you begin. Use care with hot pans, ovens and blenders, and ask an experienced adult for help when needed.
