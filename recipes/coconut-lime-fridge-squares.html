Coconut-Lime Fridge Squares

Zesty coconut squares that set in the refrigerator, no oven needed.

Beginner friendly · No oven needed

Ingredients

  • 2 cups desiccated coconut
  • 1/2 cup condensed milk
  • 1 tbsp lime juice
  • 1 tsp lime zest
  • 2 tbsp extra coconut for topping

Method

  1. Mix coconut and condensed milk in a bowl.
  2. Stir in lime juice and zest.
  3. Press firmly into a small lined container.
  4. Sprinkle extra coconut over the top.
  5. Refrigerate until firm, then cut into squares.

Kitchen note: Read the full method before you begin. Keep chilled ingredients refrigerated until serving.
