Loaded Veggie Nachos

Crunchy nachos topped with colourful vegetables.

Beginner friendly · Easy-to-follow steps

Ingredients

  • 2 cups tortilla chips
  • 1/2 cup grated cheese
  • 1/4 cup chopped tomato
  • 1/4 cup sweet corn
  • 2 tbsp sliced olives
  • Salsa to serve

Method

  1. Arrange tortilla chips in an oven-safe dish.
  2. Scatter cheese, tomato, corn and olives over the chips.
  3. Bake at 180°C for 5–7 minutes until cheese melts.
  4. Serve warm with salsa.
  5. Use an oven-safe dish and handle it carefully.

Kitchen note: Read the full method before you begin. Use care with hot pans, ovens and blenders, and ask an experienced adult for help when needed.
