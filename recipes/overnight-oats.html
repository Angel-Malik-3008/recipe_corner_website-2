Overnight Oats

A make-ahead breakfast with creamy oats.

Beginner friendly · Easy-to-follow steps

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup rolled oats
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 1/4 cup yoghurt
  • 1 tsp honey
  • Fruit and nuts for topping

Method

  1. Combine oats, milk, yoghurt and honey in a jar.
  2. Stir well and cover.
  3. Refrigerate for at least 4 hours or overnight.
  4. Stir in the morning and add fruit and nuts.
  5. Add a splash of milk if you prefer a thinner texture.

Kitchen note: Read the full method before you begin. Use care with hot pans, ovens and blenders, and ask an experienced adult for help when needed.
