Easy Chocolate Pudding

A smooth cocoa pudding that sets in the fridge.

Beginner friendly · Easy-to-follow steps

Ingredients

  • 2 cups milk
  • 2 tbsp cocoa powder
  • 3 tbsp cornflour
  • 1/4 cup sugar
  • 40 g chopped chocolate
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla

Method

  1. Whisk cocoa, cornflour, sugar and a little cold milk until smooth.
  2. Warm the remaining milk in a saucepan.
  3. Whisk in the cocoa mixture and stir over medium-low heat until thick.
  4. Remove from heat and stir in chocolate and vanilla.
  5. Pour into cups, cool and refrigerate until set.

Kitchen note: Read the full method before you begin. Use care with hot pans, ovens and blenders, and ask an experienced adult for help when needed.
