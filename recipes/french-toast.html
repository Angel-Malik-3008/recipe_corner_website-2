French Toast

Golden bread slices with a lightly spiced coating.

Beginner friendly · Easy-to-follow steps

Ingredients

  • 4 bread slices
  • 1 egg
  • 1/3 cup milk
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1/4 tsp cinnamon
  • A little butter

Method

  1. Whisk egg, milk, sugar and cinnamon in a shallow bowl.
  2. Dip each bread slice briefly on both sides.
  3. Melt a little butter in a pan over medium-low heat.
  4. Cook bread for 2–3 minutes per side until golden and set.
  5. Serve warm with fruit or yoghurt.

Kitchen note: Read the full method before you begin. Use care with hot pans, ovens and blenders, and ask an experienced adult for help when needed.
